LEDGERLENS
Reconciliation that never leaves your computer.

WHAT IS IN HERE
  ledgerlens.html            The whole tool. One file. Double-click it.
  sample-invoices.csv        Worked example - 20 invoices.
  sample-bank-statement.csv  Worked example - the matching bank export.

HOW TO USE IT
  1. Double-click ledgerlens.html. It opens in your browser.
  2. Drop in your sales/invoice export and your bank or processor export.
  3. Check the column mapping it guessed, then hit Reconcile.
  4. Fill in the client name and "Prepared by", then Print or save as PDF.

To rehearse the demo, click "Load the worked example" instead of loading files -
or load the two sample CSVs above by hand to show it reading real files.

THE PART WORTH SAYING OUT LOUD ON A CALL
  Nothing is uploaded. No server, no account, no internet needed after the file
  is on the machine. The client's financial data never leaves their computer,
  because the tool runs inside their own browser tab and forgets everything when
  the tab closes. That is why a stranger will run it.

NOTES
  - Reads CSV. Excel files need saving as CSV first.
  - Works offline. The only thing the internet is used for is loading the
    typeface; without it the tool still works, just in a default font.
  - CSV exports leave via "Copy to clipboard", then paste into a spreadsheet.
    Printing to PDF works normally.
