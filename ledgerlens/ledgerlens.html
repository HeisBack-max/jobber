Find the money that doesn’t line up

Two exports, side by side: what you think happened and what the bank says happened. Ledgerlens matches them line by line and reports everything that doesn’t agree — unpaid invoices, payments that arrived short, charges that landed twice, deposits with nothing behind them.

Your records

Invoices or sales — exported from your accounting system as CSV.

Bank or processor

A bank statement, or a Stripe / PayPal / Square payout export, as CSV.

Both files are read in this tab. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is saved.
