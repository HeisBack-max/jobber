What the unit does on its own

Place a body and plant a transmitter at the same time and the two channels correlate without you touching anything: the banner rewrites itself to correlated contact, the primary card turns red, and a three-tone alarm fires. Pressing 5 then 1 a few seconds apart is the whole reveal — let it escalate on its own rather than hitting both at once.

Blocking notes: the door reads slightly cooler than the wall, so it is always visible — a body behind it blooms through as a soft, diffuse shape rather than a sharp one, which is what a real through-partition read looks like. Give the box a beat to settle: confidence climbs from 0.6 to 0.9 over about two seconds. RF proximity decays on its own, so an abandoned bug goes quiet; walk back in with ↑. Audio needs one click on the page before it will sound — press A during your pre-set. With a body and a bug both live the mix gets busy on purpose; I parks the thermal channel if you need the RF clicks alone for a line.
